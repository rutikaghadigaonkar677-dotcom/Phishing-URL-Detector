About Phishing

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where attackers try to trick people into sharing sensitive information through fake websites, messages, or emails.

How Does Phishing Work?

An attacker creates a fake link or website that looks like a trusted website. When a user visits it, they may be asked to enter personal or login information.

Common Types of Phishing

📧 Email Phishing

Fake emails are used to trick users into clicking suspicious links or sharing personal information.

How to Identify Email Phishing

  • Unknown or suspicious sender addresses.
  • Urgent messages asking you to act quickly.
  • Links asking for passwords or bank details.

How to Stay Safe

Verify the sender and avoid clicking suspicious links or opening unexpected attachments.

📱 Smishing

Smishing is a phishing attack carried out through SMS or text messages.

How to Identify Smishing

  • Messages claiming you won a prize.
  • Fake parcel delivery notifications.
  • Messages requesting OTPs or personal details.

How to Stay Safe

Do not reply to suspicious messages or share OTPs. Verify requests through official channels.

🌐 Website Phishing

Fake websites are designed to look like legitimate websites to steal information.

How to Identify Website Phishing

  • Misspelled domain names.
  • Unexpected redirects or pop-ups.
  • Unusual requests for login or payment details.

How to Stay Safe

Check the website address carefully and visit trusted websites using their official URLs.
