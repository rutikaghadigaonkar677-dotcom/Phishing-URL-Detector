Frequently Asked Questions

What is a phishing website?

A phishing website is a fake website designed to trick users into sharing personal or sensitive information.

How does phishing work?

Attackers create fake websites, emails or messages that look trustworthy and try to trick users.

How can I identify a phishing website?

Check the website URL, look for spelling mistakes, suspicious links and unexpected requests for information.

How can I protect myself from phishing?

Use strong passwords, enable two-factor authentication and avoid clicking unknown or suspicious links.

What should I do if I receive a suspicious link?

Do not click the link. Verify the sender and website before providing any personal information.
