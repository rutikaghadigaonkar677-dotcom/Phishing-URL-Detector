safety Tips

Follow these simple safety tips to protect yourself from phishing attacks.

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for your online accounts.

🔗

Check Links

Always check the website address before clicking on an unknown link.

🛡️

Enable 2FA

Enable two-factor authentication to add an extra layer of security.

⚠️

Avoid Unknown Links

Do not click suspicious links received from unknown sources.

📧

Verify Emails

Verify the sender before responding to unexpected emails.
